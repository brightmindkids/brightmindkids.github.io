Good manners cards

Twelve friendly cards to help little ones learn kind habits. Print them in colour for the fridge, or as colouring pages your child can decorate. Free to print for home and classroom use.

Style:

How to print

  • Click Print the cards. You get 6 cards per A4 or Letter page (2 pages).
  • For colouring pages, choose Colouring pages first.
  • Cut along the dashed lines. Thick paper or lamination makes them last.
  • To save a PDF, choose "Save as PDF" as the printer.

Ideas to use them

  • Pick a "manner of the week" and stick the card on the fridge.
  • Act out each card with toys or puppets.
  • Notice and praise the habit when you see it: "You waited your turn!"
  • Mix the cards face down and let your child pick one to practise today.
