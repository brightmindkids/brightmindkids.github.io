Yunus and the Giant Whale

An Islamic Story and Activity Book for Kids About Prayer, Patience, and Forgiveness

Ages 4–8Paperbackby Bright Mind Kids

A gentle ocean adventure retelling the story of Prophet Yunus (AS): his journey, the giant whale, and his heartfelt dua in the dark. Children learn that Allah always hears us, and that saying sorry and asking forgiveness brings light back into our hearts. The story is followed by ocean-themed mazes, matching and counting games, and a certificate to celebrate your little reader.

What your child will learn

  • Prayer and dua: Allah always hears us
  • Patience in difficult moments
  • Saying sorry and asking forgiveness
  • Ocean mazes, matching and counting games

Includes activity pages and a certificate of achievement.
