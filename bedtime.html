Tips for a calm bedtime

  • Keep the same order every night. Children relax when they know what comes next.
  • Turn screens off a while before bed and dim the lights.
  • Let your child tick or colour each step themselves.
  • Praise the effort: "You brushed your teeth all by yourself!"

Make it your own

  • Add your child's name so the chart feels like theirs.
  • Choose a small weekly reward: picking Saturday's story or a park trip.
