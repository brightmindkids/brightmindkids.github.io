Learn the Quran

Read and listen to Al-Fatiha and the short surahs, one verse at a time. Turn on repeat to help memorise, and earn a star for every surah you learn!

For parents: the Arabic text is the Uthmani script of the King Fahd Complex (via QuranEnc). Transliteration and the English meaning (Saheeh International) come from Tanzil.net. The recitation is by Sheikh Mishary Rashid Alafasy, played from EveryAyah.com. The translation gives the meaning only; it is not the Quran itself. Please make sure your child treats the screen respectfully while reading, and check pronunciation with a teacher.
