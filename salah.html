Wudu & Salah

Learn how to get ready for prayer with wudu, then how to pray each of the five daily prayers, step by step, with the words to say.

For parents: this guide shows the steps most Muslims share. Some details differ between schools of thought, for example whether the hands are folded or kept at the sides when standing, or whether the prayer ends with one or two salams. Please teach your child the way your family and your local imam or teacher pray. The washing steps follow the verse of wudu (Surah Al-Ma’idah 5:6) and the well-known description of the Prophet’s ﷺ wudu.
